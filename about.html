About ToolBox

Learn what ToolBox is and why it was created.

Simple tools, less friction

ToolBox is a lightweight collection of everyday browser-based utilities. Version 1.0 focuses on five practical tools: QR creation, image resizing, image compression, percentage calculations, and PDF merging.

Our approach

The site is intentionally simple: no accounts, no dashboard, no database, and no unnecessary steps. The goal is to make the main task obvious and keep the project easy to maintain and expand.

Growing carefully

Additional tools may be added over time based on usefulness and visitor demand.
