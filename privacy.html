Privacy Policy

Privacy information for the ToolBox website.

Version 1.0: the image and PDF tools process selected files locally in your browser. The ToolBox application code does not upload those selected files to a ToolBox server.

Files you select

Image Resizer, Image Compressor, and PDF Merger use browser APIs to read and process the files you choose on your device. Closing or refreshing the page clears the tool's active state.

QR content and calculator values

QR generation and percentage calculations are performed by JavaScript running in your browser.

Hosting and third parties

The hosting provider may process standard network information needed to serve this website. If analytics, advertising, or other third-party services are introduced later, this policy should be updated before or when those services are enabled.

Changes

This policy may be updated as ToolBox develops. The published version on this page is the applicable website notice.
